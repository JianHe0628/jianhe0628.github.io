## About Me

I am a PhD Candidate at the [Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP)](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/centre-vision-speech-signal-processing), University of Surrey, supervised by [Professor Richard Bowden](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/richard-bowden). My research focuses on making sign language accessible to machines — building systems that can understand, translate, and produce sign language to improve communication for Deaf and hard-of-hearing communities.

**Research Interests**

<ul class="research-tags">
  <li>Sign Language Recognition</li>
  <li>Sign Language Translation</li>
  <li>Sign Language Production</li>
  <li>Large Language Models</li>
  <li>Video Understanding</li>
  <li>Temporal Segmentation</li>
</ul>
