I am a PhD candidate at the [Centre for Vision, Speech and Signal Processing (CVSSP)](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/centre-vision-speech-signal-processing), University of Surrey, supervised by [Prof. Richard Bowden](https://www.surrey.ac.uk/people/richard-bowden). My research is about making sign language accessible to machines: building systems that understand, translate and produce sign language, to improve communication for Deaf and hard-of-hearing communities. I am currently also a Research Scientist Intern at Canva Research UK.
