We design and deploy a wake word detection and speech recognition pipeline on a resource-constrained edge device to enable hands-free control of an electric wheelchair. The system achieves practical accuracy while meeting strict latency and power constraints, demonstrating that embedded AI can meaningfully improve assistive technology accessibility.
