title: "Gloss-Free Sign Language Translation: An Unbiased Evaluation of Progress in the Field"

venue: Computer Vision and Image Understanding (CVIU)
venue_short: CVIU 2025
type: journal

---

We systematically evaluate gloss-free sign language translation methods under a unified and unbiased protocol, revealing that reported gains in prior work are partially attributable to inconsistent evaluation practices. Our standardised benchmark enables fair comparison and more reliable measurement of genuine progress in the field.
